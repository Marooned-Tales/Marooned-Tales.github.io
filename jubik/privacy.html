Jubik: Word Puzzle Game – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Jubik: Word Puzzle Game ("Jubik") is a word game for Android made by Marooned Tales ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses collect, and the choices you have.

Information the game itself keeps

Jubik has no accounts and no sign-in. Your best scores, the game language, sound and music settings, tutorial progress and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved only on your device. We do not receive or store this information, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Jubik shows ads between rounds using Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Ads may be personalised based on this information where the law allows it and you have not opted out. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows Google's consent form, where you choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices at the bottom of the title screen.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Buying Remove Ads in the game stops all ads, and with them the AdMob data collection described above.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google Play only tells the game whether you own the purchase. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Jubik is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help have it removed.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the Google services named above, which process it as described here.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
